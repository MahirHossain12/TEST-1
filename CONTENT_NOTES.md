# Set 02: development projects and historical sites

This 30-question Bengali practice set is based on the development-project and archaeological/historical-site notes supplied by the user. It contains 15 questions on development projects and 15 on heritage. It is not an official BPSC question paper.

Each question has four choices, one correct answer and a short explanation. Answer positions vary. `QUESTION_SET` was changed so saved answers from Set 01 cannot be applied to this replacement set.

## Wording and factual scope

- MRT-6 distance (20.1 km) and station count (16) explicitly refer to **Uttara North–Motijheel**, excluding the Kamalapur extension.
- Rooppur's 2,400 MW refers to its two units' **design capacity**, not current generation or commissioning status. The supplied statement about Russian and Indian assistance is not used as an exam fact.
- Matarbari is described as a deep-sea port **project**, without claiming that construction is complete or that the port is operational.
- The Karnaphuli tunnel question tests its river location; the supplied rounded tunnel length is not used.
- Paharpur questions use Naogaon, Somapura, Dharmapala and the Pala dynasty. The supplied Atrai river claim is not used.
- The Shalban Vihara question uses its location in Cumilla. The supplied builder attribution is not used.
- Wari-Bateshwar's age is explicitly approximate, not a precise founding date.
- Lalbagh's construction is described as started by Prince Muhammad Azam, continued under Shaista Khan and left incomplete; it is not described as a completed fort built jointly by them.

Attempts to consult Banglapedia and UNESCO during this update were blocked by the environment's network policy. Those unavailable pages are not represented as sources that were successfully checked. This set avoids the questionable claims noted above. Review future revisions of time-sensitive infrastructure facts before changing the question set.
